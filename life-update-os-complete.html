Key Insight | 關鍵洞察

APP's real goal is not to train a person who always gets it right. It's to train a person who can quickly identify, diagnose, update, and re-act after getting it wrong.

APP真正要訓練的不是一個「永遠做對的人」。而是一個能在做錯之後快速辨識、診斷、更新、重新行動的人。

Ultimate Goal
最終目標
▼

The final goal is not to replace users' decision-making. But to let users gradually not need APP, yet can still complete: See → Diagnose → Predict → Act → Feedback → Update. At that point, APP's real product is not the answer. It's the user's own updating capability.

最終目標不是替使用者決定人生。而是讓使用者逐漸不需要APP，也能自己完成：看見→診斷→預測→行動→回饋→更新。這時APP真正生成的產品不是答案。而是使用者自己的更新能力。
