🔍 把抱怨轉成資料 | Turn Complaints Into Data
💡 什麼是「把抱怨轉成資料」？
抱怨：「我就是很懶，完全沒有毅力。」

資料：「星期一到四讀書可以持續，但星期五晚上10點後通常會拖延。遇到不會的題目時更明顯。」
Complaint: "I'm just lazy, no willpower at all."

Data: "I can focus Mon-Thu, but procrastinate after 10 PM on Fridays. It's worse when I encounter difficult problems."
📊 這個問題反覆發生嗎？| Is This Problem Recurring?
🎯 Pattern Detection
為什麼要看Pattern？

一次失敗 = 可能是意外
三次以上重複 = 暴露了系統問題

例如：你注意到每次都在晚上10點才拖延，那問題可能不是「我懶」，而是「疲勞導致啟動困難」。
Why look for patterns?

One failure = might be coincidence
Three+ repeats = exposes system issue

Example: If you notice procrastination always at 10 PM, the issue isn't "I'm lazy" but "fatigue makes it hard to start."
🔎 可能的原因診斷 | Possible Diagnoses
⚠️ 禁止直接人格化 | Avoid Personality Judgments
不要做：「我很懶」

要做：診斷機制
• 任務太大？
• 睡眠不足？
• 手機誘惑？
• 害怕失敗？
• 不知道第一步？
Don't: "I'm lazy"

Do: Diagnose the mechanism
• Task too big?
• Sleep deprived?
• Phone distraction?
• Fear of failure?
• Don't know first step?

💡 Prediction Error 是成長引擎
預測： 我只能做5分鐘
實際： 我做了28分鐘

Prediction Error = 23分鐘

這告訴你：「問題不是我沒有毅力，而是啟動困難。」
Prediction: I can only do 5 minutes
Actual: I did 28 minutes

Prediction Error = 23 minutes

This shows: "It's not willpower, it's just hard to start."
🔄 更新你的模型 | Update Your Model
你原本以為：
「我很懶，沒有毅力。」
You thought:
"I'm lazy with no willpower."
資料現在顯示：
「只要成功啟動，我通常可以持續20分鐘以上。」
Data now shows:
"Once I start, I can usually continue 20+ minutes."
新模型：
「真正瓶頸是啟動難度，不是毅力。」
New model:
"The real bottleneck is starting, not endurance."

不是「什麼都能控制」

每一次失敗都是一個更新模型的機會。
Not "I can control everything"

But "I'm no longer trapped by old beliefs"

Every failure is a chance to update your model.

📚 Skill Library Reference
✅ 問題定義Skill | Problem Definition Skill ▼
把「我人生完蛋了」變成「這次數學段考比預期低18分」
Turn "My life is ruined" into "My math midterm was 18 points lower than expected"
✅ 可控變數Skill | Controllable Variables Skill ▼
不可控：老師出什麼題
可控：錯題分析、練習量、複習時間、求助
Uncontrollable: What questions the teacher asks
Controllable: Analyze mistakes, practice volume, review time, seek help
✅ 5分鐘啟動Skill | 5-Minute Launch Skill ▼
降低開始摩擦：不要說「我要讀3小時書」，而是「我先坐到書桌5分鐘」
Lower the starting friction: Don't say "I'll study 3 hours", say "I'll sit at my desk for 5 minutes"
✅ Bottleneck Skill | Bottleneck Identification Skill ▼
英文考不好可能因為：單字差、文法差、讀不快、不會定位...每個的解法都不同。
Poor English score can come from: vocab gaps, grammar, reading speed, can't locate evidence...each has different solutions
✅ Prediction Error Skill | Prediction Error Skill ▼
Expected vs Actual → 這個差異不是失敗，是最寶貴的學習資料
Expected vs Actual → This difference isn't failure, it's your most valuable learning data
